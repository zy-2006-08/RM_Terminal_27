OPEN DECISION FOR THE USER
================================================================
F3 item 6 needs a product-code change plus a regression test. Two defensible
fixes, and this is a design call rather than a mechanical one:
  (i)  clear latest_ when the socket disconnects / the decoder is torn down,
       so the existing placard path renders naturally; or
  (ii) leave latest_ alone and have the dashboard/pane suppress a stale frame,
       painting an explicit "offline"/"frozen" status over or instead of it.
(ii) preserves the option of showing a dimmed last-known frame with an honest
overlay, which an operator may prefer to a blank placard. (i) is smaller.
Not implemented yet - it is a scope change on pre-existing behaviour and has
not been approved. F3 will be re-run in full after the decision.
