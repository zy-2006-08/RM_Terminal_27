-----------------------------------------------------------------------

simulation was added, and 146-179 now lands in match-tick logic. Audited by

  awk '/^def _on_connect/,/^    match\.apply_command = apply_command/' \
      <baseline 5437b0c> > /tmp/t14_ctrl_baseline.txt

  -> empty; 39 lines on both sides

VERDICT PASS. The control loop (_on_connect subscribe, _on_message handler for
CustomControl/CommonCommand, and the apply_command body including the 0x03
emergency stop) is BYTE-IDENTICAL to the module-1 baseline. Module 2 neither
extended nor touched it.

SCOPE DECLARATION, as the plan requires: that loop STILL EXISTS AND IS STILL
EXECUTABLE. sim/match_server.py subscribes to CustomControl and CommonCommand and
will mutate simulator state — chassis mode, auto-aim, gimbal recentre, and an
emergency stop — if anything publishes those topics. It belongs to the SIMULATOR,
not to the terminal. Module 2 does not extend it and nothing in module 2 publishes
to it.
This must not be conflated with the terminal's read-only property. "The simulator
has a control subscriber" and "the terminal is read-only" are separate facts. The
terminal's read-only property stands on its own terminal-side evidence — AUDIT 1
(no source call), AUDIT 2 (no linked symbol), and AUDIT 6 (nothing observed on the
wire) — and does not depend on the simulator's state in any way. A subscriber with
no publisher is an unused listener, not a control path out of the terminal.
